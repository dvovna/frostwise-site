What it does

  • Plot plan beds of any shape, planting by squares

  • Frost dates for your place from long-term weather, and you can correct them

  • Reminders and frost warnings at most one reminder a day

How to join the beta

  1. Leave your Gmail address below
  2. In January we send you a link to the test on Google Play
  3. Use the app for two weeks and tell us what gets in the way

Google Play lets testers in by their Google account, so we ask for a Gmail address.
